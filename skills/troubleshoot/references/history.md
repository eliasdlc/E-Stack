# Why the troubleshoot method exists

Eleven times in four months the same defect got closed at the symptom Elias
quoted and came back one step further on. On 5 June the GPS simulator was fixed
for the first stop and failed at the second. On 26 June Elias wrote "Do not try
to guess the problem, analyze it and find it".

In August, six rounds of loose hypotheses about a 403 went by without a command
that reproduced it. That is why three failed predictions send the work back to
Phase 1.
