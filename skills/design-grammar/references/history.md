# Why design-grammar says what it says

Read this only when changing a rule of the skill; it is not needed to follow them.

- Why DESIGN.md exists: on 20 Sep 2026 three demos built in one week (Repuestos Santiago, Empresas Morel, PetHospital) came out as one page: Geist from create-next-app, Tailwind slate, rounded-md, the same hairline grid. The mock gate had been skipped, so no direction was chosen and scaffold defaults filled the vacuum. Elias, 20 Sep: "the html plan is not the design, it is the place where we show the design". 22 Sep 2026: design-taste-frontend moved to vendor and its most-fired rules became references/web-checklist.md (Opus 5.5 rewrite).
- Why the taste ledger is written the same turn: Elias said "no agregues sombras", "que no parezcan hechos con IA", "no gradients" and placed the pill logo "como el de la derecha" five times in a row, because each correction lived only in one chat.
- Where the grammar came from: it was extracted from the Liora system and stripped of Liora (no palette, no fonts, no domain, no screens), so two products built on it read as siblings and not as the same app in a different color.
- Where the comments went (9 Nov 2026): the cut of SKILL.md from about 22.5K to about 11.9K moved the web `[web]` paragraphs into one Web table over `references/web-checklist.md`, DESIGN.md authoring into `references/design-md.md`, and the "skills that load before the first pixel" table out (the `design-identity` hook is its lock).
