01

Structure of a document

The fixed order, written by build.py <file.html> --new --type <type> and refused by build.py when a piece of it is missing. The recipe for the type (recipes.md) decides what goes in the body; this does not change.

  1. Sticky bar with sections, only if there are three or more. On mobile the links hide, not the bar.
  2. Header: kicker (project, type, date), an h1 that is the conclusion and not the task, a lede of 4 lines, 2 to 4 stats, and the "what I need from you" block if a decision is pending.
  3. Body in numbered sections only if they are a sequence or a ranking; otherwise, h2 with no number.
  4. Decision questions at the end, with the answers composer.
  5. Provenance: files, commits, dates, and everything estimated or invented, marked.
Golden ruleThe conclusion first, always. The h1 is the finding. If Elias only reads the header, he already knows what to decide.

03

Decision questions

Every open decision is a numbered question with options. The composer assembles the code Elias pastes in the chat. The recommendation goes first and marked.

1

The question, concrete and with context in the title (data-multi: several get picked)

The note and the X option of every question are added by the script: they do not go in the markup. A single real path: data-single with one .pick presented as taken.

Decision with components: the option IS the component

Question, problem, solution and options that show what they decide. Every component lives in a .stage: the markup that goes into the repo, at its real size, scaled to the column and never below 11 px of text.
