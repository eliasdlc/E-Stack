# Why the class skill works the way it does

## The automatic run has no gate

Elias decided it on 21 September 2026, about transcripts picked up by
the class watcher that nobody asked about: "no hay necesidad de mis permisos
en estos casos". The guard against an invented date lives in
`class_session.py save`, not in his attention, which is why the run can be
left alone.

## Fixed hours instead of every ten minutes

The timer used to fire every ten minutes. Measured on 21 September 2026: the
class of 14:05 ended at 16:00 and Teams did not publish its recording until
19:27, three and a half hours later. A sweep tied to the end of the block
arrives before the file, so the timer fires at fixed hours after the blocks,
and the 23:30 run collects what was published late.

## Where the automatic Kino rules came from

Moved here from the university workspace contract. 20 Sep 2026: the local Kino
answered 404 on /api/systems while the claude.ai connector worked; the
connector was dropped on 22 Sep 2026 to keep one Kino (an Opus 5.5 rewrite of
the contract). 21 Sep 2026, Elias on automatic class runs: "no hay necesidad de
mis permisos en estos casos".
