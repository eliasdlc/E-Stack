# Obligations and deadlines before DGII

All verified against official DGII documents (August 2026).

## The assigned obligations

On registering with activities 729201, 722001, 722003, 722002 and 744001, DGII
assigned these four obligations under the **regimen ordinario**:

| Code | What it is | When |
|---|---|---|
| 606 | Formato de Compras | first **15 days** of the following month |
| 607 | Formato de Ventas | first **15 days** of the following month |
| ITB | ITBIS, form IT-1 | day **20** of each month |
| IR1 | Renta Persona Fisica | **31 March** |

Legal basis of the formats deadline: Art. 8, Norma General 07-2018.
Example: the May period is reported by 15 June at the latest.

## Mandatory order of the monthly cycle

    606 and 607  ->  Anexo A  ->  IT-1

If the formats are not filed, the IT-1 does not add up.

## Zero filings: the most expensive mistake of the first year

A month with no operations **does not exempt you from filing**. The format has
to be sent informatively, at zero. It applies to **606, 607 and 608**.

Path: OFV -> menu **Declaraciones Juradas** -> **Declaraciones en Cero** ->
select the format -> period in `YYYYMM` -> Presentar Declaracion y/o Formato.

The system returns a reference code. Keep it.

The IT-1 is also filed even when it goes at zero.

## What changes with the RST

If DGII approves the admission to the Regimen Simplificado de Tributacion:

| Obligation | Under RST |
|---|---|
| 606, 607, 608 | **disappear** |
| Anticipos de ISR | **not paid** |
| Impuesto a los Activos | **not paid** (if they belong to the activity) |
| ITBIS and ISR | **simplified annual** return |

RST deadlines for a natural person:
- Annual return: **last working day of February** of the following year
- 1st installment: by the filing deadline at the latest
- 2nd installment: **last working day of August** of the same year

RST application: up to **60 days before the last working day of February**.
DGII answers within 15 working days.

Requirements to enter and to stay in:
- RNC data updated and correct
- Fiscal year end 31 December
- Up to date with every obligation
- No open external audit

Caps: RD$12,068,181.09 for 2026. Ley 30-26 raises it to RD$15,000,000 for
professional natural persons from 2027.

Activities excluded from the RST: real estate construction, manufacture of
cigarettes/fuel/alcoholic beverages, insurance, telecommunications, financial
services, pension fund management, securities brokerage, public shows,
management of autonomous estates.
Selling software **to** a company in those lines excludes nobody. What excludes
you is dedicating yourself to that activity.

## Electronic invoicing (e-CF)

Ley 32-23. For **small, micro and unclassified** taxpayers the deadline is
**15 November 2026** (Aviso 06-26).

Noncompliance: fine of **5 to 50 minimum wages**.

Free route:
1. OFV -> Solicitudes -> **Facturador Gratuito**
2. Request the **free digital certificate** from the Facturador itself
   (free for 1 year, only works inside the Facturador Gratuito)
3. Requirements: registered RNC, **Alta de NCF**, OFV password, not being
   authorized to issue e-CF through another system

Limit: approximately **150 vouchers per month**.

e-CF types to request:
- **31** Factura de Credito Fiscal Electronica (for companies)
- **32** Factura de Consumo Electronica (for individuals)

States of an e-CF: Aceptado (valid), Rechazado (does not exist for DGII),
En proceso, Aceptado Condicional (valid but with something to fix).

## Keeping documents

Art. 50 of the Codigo Tributario: **10 years**.

The Facturador Gratuito only keeps the e-CF for **2 years**. Download the XML
issued and received every month and back them up on your own.

Also keep the **certificado de retencion** from every client. Without that
document the 15% of withheld ISR is not credited in the IR-1.

## RNC registration procedure

Registered application code: **1194151** (August 2026).

Last step pending after Procesar Cambios: an email arrives with a link, the form
is printed, **signed by hand** and sent scanned to
`incorporacion-rnc@dgii.gov.do`. Deadline: **30 days**. Without that, the
registration is not approved. The certificate arrives about 24 hours later.
